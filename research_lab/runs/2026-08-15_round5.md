## 2. A canonical trial object: constrained Slepian extremizer

Let `P_lambda` be time restriction to `[-lambda,lambda]`, `F` the Fourier transform, and define the positive concentration-defect operator

    D_lambda = I - P_lambda F* P_lambda F P_lambda

on the time-limited space. In a prolate basis its eigenvalues are the leakage numbers

    d_{m,lambda} = 1 - |chi_{m,lambda}|^2,

ordered increasingly for the highly concentrated modes.

Restrict to the even Fourier-phase sector appropriate to the Riemann construction and impose the cancellation functional `ell(f)=0` (at minimum the vanishing-integral constraint used in the construction of the Riemann trial vector; additional Poisson-cancellation constraints can be incorporated by the same method).

Define the **constrained Slepian extremizer**

    h_lambda^* = argmin { <D_lambda f,f> : ||f||=1, ell(f)=0 }.

This object is canonical and does not truncate a priori to the first two prolate modes.

## 3. PROVED ABSTRACT FACT: rank-one constrained concentration

Let `D` be selfadjoint with simple increasing eigenvalues

    d0 < d1 < d2 < ...

and normalized eigenvectors `e_j`. Let `ell(f)=<a,f>` be a nonzero bounded functional and assume `a0=<e0,a>` and `a1=<e1,a>` are nonzero.

The lowest eigenvalue `nu` of the compression of `D` to `ker ell` is simple, lies in `(d0,d1)`, and is characterized by the secular equation

    sum_j |a_j|^2/(d_j-nu) = 0.

A corresponding eigenvector is

    f_nu = c sum_j conjugate(a_j)/(d_j-nu) e_j.

### Proof

The Euler–Lagrange equation for minimizing `<Df,f>` under `||f||=1` and `ell(f)=0` is

    (D-nu)f = c a.

Expanding in the eigenbasis gives the displayed formula. The constraint gives the secular equation. On `(d0,d1)` its left side is strictly increasing, tends to `-infinity` at `d0+` and `+infinity` at `d1-`, hence has exactly one root. Rank-one compression interlacing then identifies it as the simple constrained ground state.

This is standard rank-one spectral compression in substance; novelty is not claimed. Its use here is to replace the ad hoc two-mode truncation by a canonical uncertainty extremizer.
