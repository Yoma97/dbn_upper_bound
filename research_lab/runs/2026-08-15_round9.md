## 0. Current literature input and caution

A current preprint, arXiv:2607.02828v2 (13 Aug 2026), gives an exact finite Guinand–Weil dictionary for the Connes–van Suijlekom / Connes–Consani–Moscovici Galerkin matrices. At fixed prime cutoff `c>1`, put

    L = log c,  Delta = L/(2*pi).

For a real even Galerkin vector `v=(v_0,...,v_N)`, embed it as symmetric coefficients `u_{-m}=u_m` and define

    T_v(t) = sum_{m=-N}^N u_m exp(2*pi*i*m*t),

    K_v(omega) = 2 int_0^omega T_v(t) T_v(omega-t) dt.

The single-frequency source

    psi_{alpha,omega}(x) = (alpha/pi) sin(2*pi*omega*x)

has quadratic value

    <v,Q_{alpha,omega}v> = alpha K_v(omega).

For a prime power `q=p^a<=c`, the prime block has

    alpha_q = -Lambda(q)/sqrt(q),
    omega_q(L) = 1 - log(q)/L.

Thus each prime power enters the finite form at its threshold `L_q=log q` through the single-frequency form `-Lambda(q)/sqrt(q) * K_v(omega_q)`.

The derivations below use only this explicit single-frequency formula and elementary Taylor/convolution analysis, so the internal theorem does not depend on the unverified numerical conclusions of the preprint.

# 1. PROVED: Prime-Threshold Jet Theorem

Let `v` be a nonzero real even Galerkin vector and let

    nu(v) = ord_{t=0} T_v(t).

Because `T_v` is even, `nu` is an even nonnegative integer. Write

    T_v(t) = a_nu t^nu + O(t^(nu+2)),
    a_nu = T_v^(nu)(0)/nu!.

Then as `omega -> 0+`,

    K_v(omega)
      = 2 int_0^omega T_v(t)T_v(omega-t)dt
      = 2 a_nu^2 B(nu+1,nu+1) omega^(2nu+1)
        + O(omega^(2nu+3)).

Since

    B(nu+1,nu+1) = (nu!)^2/(2nu+1)!,

we obtain the exact leading law

    K_v(omega)
      = [2 |T_v^(nu)(0)|^2/(2nu+1)!] omega^(2nu+1)
        + O(omega^(2nu+3)).

In particular, for every fixed nonzero `v`,

    K_v(omega) > 0

for all sufficiently small `omega>0`.

For a prime power `q` entering at `L_q=log q`, put `delta=L-L_q>0`. Since

    omega_q(L) = 1 - L_q/L
               = delta/L_q + O(delta^2),

the new prime contribution satisfies

    Delta_q Q_L[v]
      = - Lambda(q)/sqrt(q) * K_v(omega_q(L))

      = - [2 Lambda(q) |T_v^(nu)(0)|^2]
          /[sqrt(q)(2nu+1)!(log q)^(2nu+1)]
          * delta^(2nu+1)
        + O(delta^(2nu+2)).

Therefore the first nonzero one-sided threshold jet is always negative:

    d_+^(2nu+1)/dL^(2nu+1) Delta_q Q_L[v] |_(L=L_q)
      = - 2 Lambda(q) |T_v^(nu)(0)|^2
          /[sqrt(q)(log q)^(2nu+1)]
      < 0.

This is a pointwise-in-direction statement; it does **not** say that the whole prime matrix is negative semidefinite near a threshold.

# 2. PROVED: moment-neutral contact order 4s+5

Define even moments

    M_{2j}(v) = sum_{m=-N}^N u_m m^(2j).

Because

    T_v^(2j)(0) = (2*pi*i)^(2j) M_{2j}(v),

if

    M_0=M_2=...=M_{2s}=0,

then

    ord_0 T_v >= 2s+2.

If additionally `M_{2s+2} != 0`, then `nu=2s+2` and

    K_v(omega)
      = [(2*pi)^(4s+5)/(pi(4s+5)!)]
        M_{2s+2}(v)^2 omega^(4s+5)
        + O(omega^(4s+7)).

Hence a new prime power is invisible on the moment-neutral sector through order `4s+4`, and the first surviving threshold jet is a negative rank-one square in the next even moment.

Equivalently, on the filtration

    H_s = {v : M_0=M_2=...=M_{2s}=0},

the prime threshold has contact order at least `4s+5`.

This provides an exact arithmetic explanation for very flat threshold behavior on deeply moment-neutral sectors.

# 3. Equivalent convolution-jet interpretation

The moment formulation is secondary. The structural statement is simply:

> Volterra self-convolution doubles the vanishing order and adds one.

If `ord_0 T=nu`, then

    ord_0 [2(T*T)_Volterra] = 2nu+1,

and the first coefficient is a positive square times a beta integral.

Thus the prime-threshold filtration is not peculiar to zeta; it is a general convolution-jet law. Zeta enters through the facts that prime powers appear as source atoms and their coefficient is `-Lambda(q)/sqrt(q)`.

# 4. DESTROYER: no uniform semidefinite prime monotonicity

A tempting but false inference would be:

    K_v(omega)>0 for every fixed v and sufficiently small omega
      => Q_{1,omega} is positive semidefinite for all sufficiently small omega.

This is false because the optimizing direction may depend on `omega` and cancel successive jets.

Already in the even sector with `N=1`, the single-frequency matrix is

    Q_even(omega) =
      [[2 omega, sqrt(2) sin(2*pi*omega)/pi],
       [sqrt(2) sin(2*pi*omega)/pi,
        2 omega cos(2*pi*omega)+sin(2*pi*omega)/pi]].

Its determinant has the expansion

    det Q_even(omega)
      = -(112*pi^4/45) omega^6
        + (128*pi^6/105) omega^8
        + O(omega^10).

Since the (0,0) entry is positive, the determinant is negative for all sufficiently small positive `omega`; therefore the matrix is indefinite arbitrarily close to the threshold.

So there is **no** prime-only Loewner monotonicity or prime-only positive-semidefinite threshold increment to exploit.

This kills the naive strategy

    each new prime preserves positivity by itself.

# 5. New structural interpretation: two different notions of threshold behavior

The threshold has two simultaneous properties:

1. **Fixed-direction law:** every fixed nonzero direction sees a negative prime contribution at its first nonzero jet.
2. **Optimized-matrix law:** the complete single-prime matrix is indefinite arbitrarily close to the threshold because `v=v(omega)` can move through the jet filtration and exploit higher-order terms.

This distinction is essential. It explains why numerical spectral paths can be extremely smooth through prime thresholds without the prime block itself possessing a useful sign.

# 6. Consequence for the RH strategy

Any successful positivity/no-crossing theorem for the finite Weil matrices must be **compensated**. The relevant object is the total form

    Q_total(L) = Q_prime(L)+Q_pole(L)+Q_arch(L),

not the prime block separately.

The prime-threshold theorem shows that the arithmetic atom is locally destabilizing along every fixed direction, while the matrix-level atom is indefinite. Hence a proof must discover a structural compensation law involving the archimedean and pole pieces, or a new invariant not based on monotonicity of the individual blocks.

---

# 7. Smallest current candidate: Null-Cone Compensation / Crossing-Form Theorem

Let `Q_N(L)` denote the cutoff-free finite Weil Galerkin matrix in the even sector. A standard first-crossing argument says that loss of positivity can occur only at some `L_*` where

    Q_N(L_*) >= 0

and the kernel is nontrivial.

A sufficient local barrier is therefore a theorem of the following arithmetic type:

> **Null-Cone Compensation Theorem (candidate).** Whenever `0 != v in ker Q_N(L_*)`, the first nonzero coefficient in the one-sided expansion of
>
>     <v,Q_N(L_*+h)v>
>
> is strictly positive after the complete prime + pole + archimedean contributions are combined, with the appropriate higher-order crossing form used at prime-power thresholds.

If this held along a path beginning in a rigorously positive base regime, a first loss of positive semidefiniteness would be impossible.

This statement is not yet proved and may be too strong. It is deliberately formulated on the **null cone only**, not as global Weil positivity.

# 9. Relation to the Laguerre / heat-flow route

The uploaded Csordas sources emphasize that the first Laguerre inequality alone is not sufficient, while the full generalized Laguerre hierarchy characterizes Laguerre–Polya membership. They also isolate positive-definiteness of associated kernels as a central open difficulty.

The prime-threshold jet filtration is structurally analogous to the boundary-jet filtration found in Round 8:

    boundary truncation  -> endpoint jets -> Bernoulli boundary symbol,
    prime threshold      -> moment jets   -> Volterra/Beta threshold symbol.

This suggests a broader new theory of **arithmetic jet filtrations**, in which local failure modes are organized by the first nonvanishing jet and the arithmetic transform converts that jet into a universal finite-dimensional form.

This theory is potentially useful beyond RH (Poisson summation, finite explicit-formula matrices, lattice sampling, and convolution-source operators). Literature novelty of this packaging remains to be audited.
