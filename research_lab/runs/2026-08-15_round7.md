## 1. Destroyer correction: the weighted absolute-tail route is too strong for raw time truncation

Fix the self-dual Fourier convention

    fhat(xi)=int_R f(x) exp(-2 pi i x xi) dx.

Let `f` be even and `C^2` on `[-L,L]`, and extend it by zero outside this interval. If `f(L) != 0`, then integration by parts gives

    fhat(xi)

Indeed, on a fixed positive-density subset of each period where `|sin(2 pi L xi)|` is bounded below, the leading term is comparable to `1/xi` and the O(xi^-2) remainder cannot cancel it for large xi.

Thus the weighted absolute Fourier-tail condition used as a sufficient estimate in Round 6 is generally inapplicable to a raw compactly truncated prolate state. The arithmetic summation must exploit oscillation rather than absolute weighted integrability.
